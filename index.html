The mission

The first 72 hours decide too much.

When someone walks out of prison with nowhere to sleep that night, every other part of reentry — finding work, continuing treatment, reconnecting with family — gets harder. Stable housing in the first days after release is the foundation everything else is built on. We provide that foundation: a safe bed, clear structure, and people who understand what the walk back looks like.

The program

Homes designed for the work of reentry.

Our homes are ordinary houses run to an exacting standard — the structure residents need, in the neighborhoods where rebuilding a life is possible.

01

Structured single-family homes

Well-maintained homes in approved jurisdictions, configured for community living with clear house standards: safety, accountability, and mutual respect. Every resident knows what is expected — and what support looks like.

02

Peer-informed operations

Our homes are run by people who understand reentry firsthand — not as a theory, but as lived experience. Residents are met with credibility, not condescension, from day one.

03

Stability as the product

We don't do treatment, job placement, or case management — we do the thing those services depend on: a stable address. Our role is to hold the foundation steady while residents and their support teams build on it.

04

Full regulatory compliance

Vendor orientation, property approval, and community notification requirements handled before a single resident arrives. We operate the way the state needs its housing partners to operate.

Who we are

Operated by people who know both sides.

Breakwater was founded on two kinds of expertise. Our founders know reentry from lived experience — poverty, recovery, and the long walk back. They know this population because they were this population.

They also know how to build and run operations that hold up under scrutiny — systems, standards, and accountability, executed professionally. That combination is rare: operators who understand the human reality of the residents and know how to run a safe, compliant, well-managed house.

Washington's own behavioral health system recognizes lived experience as a professional credential. We built a company on that principle.

"A breakwater doesn't mark the way — it stands between the storm and the harbor and takes the hit. That's the job."
