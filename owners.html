Leadership

Breakwater Reentry Housing is owned and operated by Kevin Mesiab and Heather Hewett.

Kevin Mesiab

Co-Owner

Kevin is a software engineer and founder with more than twenty years of experience building and operating companies, from hardware research and development through venture-backed startups to independent software products.

He knows what it means to start over — and he knows that the difference between making it and not is usually one stable address and one person who doesn’t give up on you.

He oversees operations and technology at Breakwater: property systems, resident intake and documentation, and vendor compliance, applying the operational discipline of a career spent shipping products on schedule.

Heather Hewett

Co-Owner

Heather has seventeen years of experience as the office manager of a medical practice, where she is responsible for insurance, finance, and daily operations.

She leads finance, insurance, and resident services at Breakwater: voucher documentation, cost management, and the administrative systems that keep each home funded and compliant. Her background in healthcare administration provides direct experience with the documentation standards and regulatory detail this work requires.
