Ownership

The people behind Breakwater

Breakwater Reentry Housing is owned and operated by Kevin Mesiab and Heather Hewett — a founder who has lived the reentry journey and an operations leader who has spent her career keeping organizations running.

Co-Owner

Kevin Mesiab

Founder · Operations & Technology

Kevin is a software engineer and serial founder who grew up in poverty. He knows what it means to start over — and he knows that the difference between making it and not is usually one stable address and one person who doesn't give up on you.

He brings two decades of building and running companies — from hardware R&D to venture-backed startups to solo software products — to the operational side of Breakwater: property systems, resident intake, compliance documentation, and the kind of process discipline that keeps a house safe and a vendor relationship solid.

Founder — multiple ventures 20+ yrs engineering & operations Washington resident
Co-Owner

Heather Hewett

Finance · Insurance · Resident Services

Heather has spent 17 years as the office manager of a medical practice, where she runs insurance, finance, and the daily operations patients depend on. She is the person who makes sure the bills are right, the paperwork is filed, and nobody falls through the cracks.

At Breakwater she leads finance, insurance, and resident services — the back-office backbone that keeps homes funded, compliant, and running. Her experience navigating insurance systems and managing household-scale budgets translates directly to the voucher documentation and cost discipline this work demands.

17 yrs office management Insurance & finance Operations leadership
